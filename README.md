# Rigged?

Most difficult decisions do not come with an obvious answer. They come with a bunch of things that matter, a few choices that are annoyingly close, and the suspicion that you may already be leaning toward one of them.

Rigged? is my attempt to make that mess a little easier to examine.

You create the options, choose your own criteria, decide how important each one is, and score what is in front of you. The app gives you a weighted ranking—but that is only half the point.

The interesting question is: **how easy would it be to change the winner?**

If a tiny adjustment to one priority flips the result, Rigged? should tell you. If the answer survives every reasonable change, it should tell you that too. I do not want the math to make the decision for anyone; I want it to expose what is actually making the decision.

## What I want it to feel like

This should not feel like filling out a spreadsheet or using a corporate analytics tool. I want it to feel quick, tactile, and slightly confrontational—in a useful way.

Move one weight and watch the others give way. Score the choices. See the answer change. Then get one clear explanation of what is driving it.

The first version will include:

- decisions with two or more options
- completely user-defined criteria
- weights that always total 100%
- simple 1–10 scoring
- a live weighted ranking
- sensitivity analysis for the closest result-changing weight shift
- a plain-language explanation of the result
- browser-based saving with no account
- mobile support and light/dark themes


## Why the name?

Every decision matrix is influenced by the weights you choose. Sometimes that is thoughtful prioritization. Sometimes it is a very convincing way to justify the answer you already wanted.

So—was the result reasoned, or was it rigged?
